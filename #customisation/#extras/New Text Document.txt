= How to enable a customisation =

- Drag a .res file into the "enabled" folder
———————————————————————————————————————————
= Available customisations =

- currently_equipped.res = Shows "Currently Equipped:" in Class Loadout
- character_info_and_setup.res = Shows "Character Info And Setup" title in Loadout
- mann_co_catalog.res = Shows "Mann Co. Catalog" title in Store
- replays_and_movies.res = Shows "Replays And Movies" title in Replays
