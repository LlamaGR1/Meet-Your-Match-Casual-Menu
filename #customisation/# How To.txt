= How to enable a customisation =

- Drag a .res file into the "enabled" folder
- Only include 1 with the same first word (e.x. don't include both scoreboard_old.res & scoreboard_vanilla)
———————————————————————————————————————————
= Available customisations =

- main_menu_party.res = Meet Your Match Main Menu with "Friends" & "Messages" buttons

- main_menu_no_party.res = Meet Your Match Main Menu without "Friends" & "Messages" buttons, to access the "Matchmaking Settings", click the hidden button next to the "Play" text
================================================================
- scoreboard_vanilla.res = Vanilla Scoreboard with some bugfixes

- scoreboard_old.res = Pre-Tough Break Scoreboard
================================================================
- class_loadout_vanilla.res = Vanilla Loadout

- class_loadout_master.res = Adds the ability to see Passive Attributes & switch to team RED & BLU
================================================================
- advanced_options_vanilla.res = Vanilla Advanced Options with some bugfixes

- advanced_options_tf2c.res = Team Fortress 2 Classic Advanced Options style
